ACME GLOBAL TECHNOLOGIES - CORPORATE TRAVEL & EXPENSE POLICY
Document ID: POL-TRAV-2026-V2

1. Purpose and Scope:
This policy establishes guidelines and allowable spending limits for all employees traveling on authorized company business.

2. Booking and Transportation:
- All flights must be booked through the Acme Travel Portal at least 14 days prior to departure.
- Standard economy class is required for domestic flights and international flights under 6 hours in duration.
- Business class is permitted only for international flights exceeding 6 consecutive hours, subject to VP approval.

3. Lodging Limits:
- Standard hotel room rates are reimbursed up to $250 per night (before local taxes) in primary metropolitan areas.
- For non-metropolitan locations, the maximum reimbursement is $180 per night.
- Incidental hotel expenses (mini-bar, pay-per-view movies) are personal and non-reimbursable.

4. Daily Meal and Per-Diem Allowance:
- Employees receive a meal allowance of up to $75 per day ($15 Breakfast, $25 Lunch, $35 Dinner).
- Receipts are mandatory for all meal expenses exceeding $25.
- Alcoholic beverages during solo business travel will not be reimbursed.

5. Expense Reporting and Reimbursement:
- Expense reports must be submitted via the Finance Portal within 14 calendar days of returning from travel.
- Approved expense reimbursements are paid within two standard payroll cycles.
